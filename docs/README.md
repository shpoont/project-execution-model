# Repository Guide

Readers looking for the finished model can start in
[deliverables](../deliverables/README.md). This guide connects the information
used to maintain it. The [project agreement](../PROJECT.md) states the purpose,
expectations and boundaries of that work.

## Current Work and Agreements

[Open issues](https://github.com/shpoont/project-execution-model/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen)
identify proposed work. [Pull requests](https://github.com/shpoont/project-execution-model/pulls)
carry implementation proposals, reviews and acceptance checks. Read the relevant
issue and pull request together, following their link to the accepted agreement,
to recover the current scope, approach, dependencies, findings and next actions.
Proposed changes are distinct from accepted ones. Their current records take precedence
over an old status summary; no second activity ledger is maintained here.

## Approach and Working Materials

The [contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) connects preparation, review and
adoption. A proposal's issue and pull request hold its particular design and
agreement. Draft passages, sample outputs and comparisons are review materials;
their results and limits stay linked to the affected work.

The maintained Markdown result lives in [deliverables](../deliverables/README.md)
with its images. It is both editable source and published output, so there is
one maintained model. [src](../src/README.md) contains the separate editable
diagram source. More detailed records for individual parts are added only when
the work needs them.

## Checks, Evidence and Decisions

- [Checks](../tests/README.md): how to assess text, links, images and publication.
- [Evidence](../evidence/README.md): selected results and their supporting sources.
- [Decision log](decision-log.md): lasting decisions, reasons and superseded directions.

Checks needed for an increment belong with its agreement. Results identify
what was actually assessed and what remains uncertain; they do not establish
untested claims or automatically authorize publication.

## Delivery and Continuing Responsibility

The [delivery guide](delivery.md) explains published files, versions, acceptance
and release verification. The [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) summarizes released
changes. Findings from model use return through issues and may reopen an earlier
decision; a successful release does not prove improved project outcomes.
