# Evidence and Supporting Records

Evidence connects a claim to what was actually observed or checked. Keep the
relevant question, source or version, conditions, method, result and limits
together. Distinguish original observations, interpretations, corrections and
remaining uncertainty.

The [repository adoption record](project-structure-adoption.md) covers the
source and navigation checks for moving the result into `deliverables/`.
For model changes, pull requests retain public review summaries and check
results alongside the exact proposed source. Earlier examples include the
[readability revision](https://github.com/shpoont/project-execution-model/pull/32)
and [small shelf example](https://github.com/shpoont/project-execution-model/pull/29).

Reference existing public records instead of copying their full histories.
Include selected artifacts when they materially support a claim and can be
published appropriately. Private conversations, personal paths and operational
receipts stay outside this public repository; no public instruction depends on
access to those private records.

Release notes report the scope and limits of the evidence. A successful check,
editorial review or publication does not establish untested model benefits.
